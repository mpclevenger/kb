The initiative tracker follows major IT initiatives that affect risk and controls, from intake to close. Each initiative has a checklist for each stage.

## Who it's for

Program and project managers, system owners and control owners working on a major change.

## How to use it

- See where each initiative is, and what is needed to move to the next stage.
- Each checklist item links to the [Initiative lifecycle](/guides/initiative-lifecycle/) guide, which explains what good evidence looks like.
