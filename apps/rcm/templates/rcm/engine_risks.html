flowchart LR
  accTitle: How the engine rates a risk
  accDescr: A questionnaire answer switches on a driver. Drivers feed a risk as likelihood drivers or impact drivers. The risk's scoring rules read those drivers to give the risk rating.
  A[Questionnaire answer] --> B[Driver switched on, RD01 and so on]
  B --> C[Likelihood drivers]
  B --> D[Impact drivers]
  C --> E[Scoring rules]
  D --> E
  E --> F[Risk rating]
